About Codefest Hackathon

Codefest Hackathon 2026 is a national-level hackathon organized by code for change . its provencial phase for chitwan 2026 is being held at Forbes College bharatpur Chitwan and we forbes Cyber Crime and Security Awareness Club are please to be its organizing commete for the year. This competition brings together talented developers, cybersecurity enthusiasts, and innovative minds from across the nation to solve real-world problems through technology and secure coding practices.

Code of Conduct

All participants and volunteers must follow the Code of Conduct of Forbes College and Codefest along with maintaining proper student decorum at all times.

  • Smoking, vaping, alcohol, and intoxicating substances are strictly prohibited anywhere on the college premises. Anyone found using or possessing them may be removed from the event and campus.
  • To maintain the safety and privacy of female participants and volunteers, only individuals whose legal identification identifies them as she/her (female) are permitted to enter Building A (Administration Building).
  • The judges' decisions are final and are not subject to interference by the organizing committee or Forbes College administration.
  • Any complaints or disputes must first be reported to the PR Lead of CFC Chitwan.
  • Photos and videos will be captured during the event for promotional purposes and may be published on the official Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn pages of Forbes College and the organizers.
  • After registration, participants may not leave the college premises before the event concludes without prior authorization from the organizing committee.
  • Everyone is responsible for protecting the college's physical infrastructure. Anyone who intentionally damages college property will be liable for the full repair or replacement cost.
  • Medical emergencies must be reported immediately to the medical team. Participants should disclose any serious allergies or medical conditions during registration or as early as possible.
  • A help desk will be available throughout the event for assistance and general inquiries.
  • Respect for every individual's privacy, dignity, and well-being is mandatory. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, intimidation, or physical violence will not be tolerated.
  • Forbes College reserves the right to remove or temporarily detain any participant or volunteer who poses a threat to the safety or security of others until the appropriate authorities or guardians are informed, in accordance with applicable law and institutional policy.
  • Follow instructions issued by event volunteers, organizers, security personnel, and college administration.
  • Keep your valuables secure. The organizers are not responsible for the loss of personal belongings.
  • Emergency exits and safety equipment must not be blocked or misused.
  • One must accept the code of conduct by entering/registering at the hackathon registration desk.
