About CCSA

Forbes Cyber Crime and Security Awareness Club (CCSA) is a Cyber security awareness club dedicated to awaring and empowering students and the community with cybersecurity knowledge and technical workshops. We strive to create a safer digital environment through education, awareness, and practical training.

Founded in 2075 BS at Forbes College, CCSA has been at the forefront of promoting cybersecurity awareness among students and fostering a community of tech-savvy individuals who are equipped to tackle modern digital challenges.
